# Vello: product brief for the website draft

## What Vello is
A consumer medicine-delivery website for India. It closes the gap between a doctor's prescription and having the medicine in hand: delivery within hours from a licensed local pharmacy, exactly what was prescribed (no silent brand swap), every order checked by a registered pharmacist, and care afterwards (leaflets, dose reminders, refills).

## Who it is for
- People managing a long-term condition who refill the same medicines every month: diabetes, blood pressure, thyroid, heart.
- Adult children ordering for parents.
- Anyone who has been made to feel awkward asking for a medicine at a counter: sexual health, mental health, periods, skin and hair.

## Launch scope
- Small curated stock, around 100 SKUs across eight concerns (see docs/catalog-seed.md).
- Prescription medicines delivered in one city. The spec's reference data uses Ludhiana (Sharma Medicos, Model Town, 1410xx pincodes); if the launch city is different, change the reference data and the seeded pincodes together so the site never contradicts itself. Non-prescription items pan-India later.
- Two doors, equal weight: order on the website, or send a prescription or a list on WhatsApp.
- Prices are printed MRPs. No discounts, no MRP-versus-ours, no coupons.
- Medicines are shown typographically (brand, molecule, strength, form, pack). No pack-shot photography.

## What the landing page must do in five seconds
1. Say what Vello is: medicine exactly as prescribed, delivered in hours, checked by a pharmacist.
2. Let someone search a medicine immediately (the hero action).
3. Offer WhatsApp as the other door, at equal dignity.
4. Feel calm, honest, unembarrassing, quietly premium.

## The three tests every screen passes
1. Dignity: could someone hold this open on a bus without embarrassment?
2. Honesty: is the price and the substitution choice stated plainly, with nothing engineered to push a decision?
3. Calm: remove one more element. Does the screen still work? Then remove it.

## Reference points (borrow the move, not the look)
- Ro: concern-first entry ("What can we help with?"), plain-language pricing, named clinicians.
- Hims: typographic confidence, the product treated as a designed object, conditions named plainly in the main navigation.
- Alto Pharmacy: pharmacist-forward trust, "we handle the hassle", warm and unhurried.
Vello is quieter than all three.

## Non-goals for this draft
Accounts, doctor consult, live payments, real inventory, ABDM linking, Hindi (but every layout must tolerate 30% longer strings).
