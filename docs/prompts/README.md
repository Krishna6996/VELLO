# Prompt sequence

Run in this order, one at a time. After each, do the review note before saying "next".

## 00-scaffold.md
**scaffold, tokens, fonts, shell**

Review after: open /styleguide. Check hex values against the spec. Check the header at 390px. If Cursor used #FFFFFF anywhere, or added a shadow to the header, fix it now; it will propagate.

## 01-primitives-vocabulary.md
**primitives and the visual vocabulary**

Review after: the seal and the blister strip are the two things people will remember. Spend time here. If the seal looks like a checkmark badge, it's wrong; it should look like a rubber stamp your family chemist owns.

## 02-catalog-search.md
**catalog model, seed, queries, search**

Review after: read ten random SKU descriptions. If any says "effective", "powerful", "cure" or "best", rewrite in the voice. This copy will be indexed by Google and read by someone's mother.

## 03-landing.md
**the landing page**

Review after: this is the page that has to feel like what you are. Cover the right half of the hero with your hand: does the left half alone say "medicine, exactly as prescribed, searchable now"? Then read every string on the page aloud. Anything that sounds like a startup wrote it gets cut.

## 04-search.md
**search**

Review after: type your mother's medicines. If any one of them returns nothing or the wrong molecule, fix the index before moving on. Search is the product on a small-stock site.

## 05-product.md
**product page**

Review after: the molecule line must be the second thing your eye lands on after the brand. If the price is louder than the molecule line, rebalance.

## 06-concerns.md
**concern hubs and the catalog listing**

Review after: the sexual-health and mind-sleep hubs are where the stigma work happens. They should look exactly as calm as the diabetes hub. Same layout, same weight, same tone. If they look different in any way, that difference is the stigma.

## 07-cart-checkout.md
**cart, prescription upload, checkout**

Review after: the two payment rows must be visually identical in weight. The two delivery rows likewise. If one is nudged, that's a dark pattern in a pharmacy, which is the exact thing Vello exists to not be.

## 08-whatsapp.md
**the WhatsApp door**

Review after: open a wa.me link on your phone. Read the prefilled message as the pharmacist would receive it. It should give them everything they need in the first line.

## 09-order-status.md
**order status and the seal**

Review after: the stamp landing on "Verified by pharmacist" is the signature moment of the whole product. Watch it ten times. It should feel like something happened, once, and then be still.

## 10-guides.md
**guides (editorial)**

Review after: read the metformin guide as a patient, then as a regulator. If it steers toward buying, cut the steer. The guide earns trust by being the most useful page on the subject, not by selling.

## 11-motion.md
**motion system and the signature moment**

Review after: scroll the landing page slowly. Nothing should move except the seal, once. If your eye is pulled anywhere else, remove that motion.

## 12-states.md
**empty and failure states**

Review after: state 2 is where most pharmacies would slip in a quiet upsell. Yours must not.

## 13-accessibility.md
**accessibility, large type, text growth**

Review after: turn on large type and pseudo-locale together. Walk through checkout on a 390px viewport. That's a 60-year-old on a budget Android phone reading Hindi next year.
